## Third-party data

The compiled tables carry their sources' terms as well:

- **Marketplace benchmarks** (`src/benchmarks.js`): built from the CMS plan-year public use
  files, US federal government works in the public domain.
- **Members and portraits**: `unitedstates/congress-legislators` and `unitedstates/images`,
  both dedicated to the public domain under CC0.
- **ZIP to congressional district** (`src/reps.js`): derived from
  `OpenSourceActivismTech/us-zipcodes-congress` (`zccd.csv`), which is built from US Census
  Bureau files and states no license of its own.
- **Cosponsors**: govinfo BILLSTATUS, US federal government works in the public domain.

Fonts (Newsreader and Hanken Grotesk, SIL Open Font License) are loaded from Google Fonts and
are not part of this repository.
